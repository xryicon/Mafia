`CommodityArtwork` selects the image by the database commodity ID. Text labels stay database-driven; future commodities use the generic inventory icon until artwork is added. Responsive WebP sources avoid shipping original PNGs to the browser. Artwork is decorative alongside each commodity’s name.

Used in fixed-price listings, auctions, the sell form, purchase/bid confirmation, inventory, funded buy orders, market summaries and the dashboard’s market/production rows. No economic rules, fees, values or records changed.

## Prompt set

### whiskey

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one square premium inventory artwork for Blackwater Mafia, a 1920s noir browser crime-economy strategy game. Highly detailed cinematic, painterly realism matching a dark industrial harbor at night. Three-quarter isometric product view, centered and fully contained with 15% breathing room, readable silhouette even at small thumbnail sizes. Background is near-black blue charcoal (#080f11), subtle cool smoke and a soft grounded shadow only. Warm muted copper-gold key light from the upper left, restrained steel-blue rim light, worn believable materials. Elegant, gritty and premium, not cartoon, not glossy mobile-game candy. No text, labels, logos, border, user interface, characters, weapons or unrelated props. Square 1024x1024 composition. Subject: WHISKEY CRATES: two weathered dark oak slatted shipping crates stacked at an angle and a smaller open wooden crate in front. Open crate reveals a neat row of squat amber glass whiskey bottles with dark cork tops and unprinted cream paper wraps. One bottle beside the crate helps instant recognition. Rich wood grain, iron corner fasteners, subtle amber reflections. Crates and bottles occupy about 70% of the frame.
